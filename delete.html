Delete your ECHO data

App: ECHO: Your Past Is Your Enemy · Developer: Tech4U · Last updated: November 10, 2026

What ECHO stores about you

ECHO has no user accounts. When you finish a Daily Challenge, the game saves the following on the world leaderboard:

  • your nickname (for example "Pilot-4821" or a name you chose)
  • your score, the round you reached, and the ship you flew
  • the time of the submission
  • a random anonymous player ID

How to request deletion

  1. Open ECHO, tap 🏆 WORLD RANKING, and note your nickname exactly as it appears in the "Your name" field.
  2. Send an email to techforu77@gmail.com with the subject "ECHO data deletion".
  3. In the email, include your nickname and the dates you played the Daily Challenge, if you remember them.

We will delete your leaderboard entries within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: every leaderboard entry under your nickname or player ID (nickname, scores, rounds, ships and timestamps), and your anonymous player ID.
  • Kept: nothing. We keep no backups or copies of deleted entries.
  • On your phone: your high scores, unlocked ships and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling ECHO or clearing its app data removes them, along with your anonymous ID.
