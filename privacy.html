ECHO — Privacy Policy

Developer: Tech4U · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

ECHO collects almost nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is your Daily Challenge score and the nickname you choose, which are used for the public world leaderboard. The game itself works offline.

Data we collect

When you finish a Daily Challenge, ECHO sends the following to the world leaderboard:

  • your nickname, which you choose and can change at any time (by default a random name such as "Pilot-4821")
  • your score, the round you reached, and the ship you flew
  • the time the score was submitted
  • a random anonymous player ID, so that only you can update your own score

We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts or location. Your nickname and score are public, because anyone can see the leaderboard, so please don't use your real name if you don't want to.

The leaderboard runs on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes this data for us under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Like any internet service, Firebase receives your device's IP address when the game connects to it. The data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell or share it with anyone else.

The Android app requests only the internet permission, which it needs for the leaderboard.

Data stored on your device

Your high scores are saved locally on your own device so the game can show your best score. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see or access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, ECHO opens your device's standard share menu with a short text summary of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Whatever happens after that is governed by the app you choose.

Web version

The browser version is hosted on GitHub Pages and may load a font from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google may process standard technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The game itself does not collect anything.

Deleting your data

Each day starts a new leaderboard. To delete your scores, email us your nickname and roughly when you played, and we will remove them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes your anonymous ID from your phone.

Children

ECHO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
