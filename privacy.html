ECHO — Privacy Policy

Developer: Tech4U · Last updated: November 1, 2026

Summary

ECHO does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline.

Data we collect

None. ECHO has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The Android app requests no permissions, including no internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Your high scores are saved locally on your own device so the game can show your best score. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see or access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, ECHO opens your device's standard share menu with a short text summary of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Whatever happens after that is governed by the app you choose.

Web version

The browser version is hosted on GitHub Pages and may load a font from Google Fonts. GitHub and Google may process standard technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The game itself does not collect anything.

Children

ECHO collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
